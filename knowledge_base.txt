VECTRA AI - PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE
===================================

PRODUCT NAME: Vectra AI
TYPE: Autonomous Multi-Agent Sales System
TARGET MARKET: Small to Medium Businesses (SMBs)

CORE FEATURES:
1. Multi-Agent Orchestration: Manager, SDR, Closer, and Finance agents work together autonomously.
2. Hybrid LLM Architecture: Uses Fireworks AI (DeepSeek-V4-Pro) for fast reasoning and Google Gemini for strict compliance auditing.
3. Double-Layer Guardrail: Combines AI Audit (Gemini) with deterministic hard rules to prevent illegal discounts, negative prices, and unrealistic promises.
4. Zero-Click Execution: Automatically logs deals and updates CRM status in Google Sheets without human intervention.
5. Glass-Box Workflow UI: Displays every agent decision (routing, negotiation, audit, execution) in real time for full transparency.
6. Memory-Aware Negotiation: CRM-native long-term memory; the AI acknowledges returning leads with full context.
7. Autonomous Outcome Detection: Deal outcomes (CLOSED / LOST / NEGOTIATING) are derived from the customer's own words, never self-reported by the AI.
8. Adaptive Personality Engine: Detects DRIVER / ANALYTICAL / AMIABLE / EXPRESSIVE communication styles and adapts tone.
9. Strategic BI (CRO Mode): Writes Chief-Revenue-Officer-level strategic reports from live CRM data.

PRICING & DISCOUNT POLICY:
- Maximum discount allowed: 10%
- No negative prices allowed
- No unrealistic promises (e.g., free lifetime upgrades, guaranteed results, uptime guarantees)
- Discounts are only offered when the customer asks about pricing or discounts
- All deals must be audited by Finance Agent before approval

BUSINESS RULES:
1. AI agents must strictly adhere to the 10% discount ceiling.
2. AI must never hallucinate product features or pricing. Vectra AI is a software-only sales automation platform: no hardware, no cybersecurity or firewall products, no guaranteed outcomes.
3. All negotiations must be logged in Google Sheets for audit trail.

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE:
- Ultra-lean architecture: No expensive vector DBs or bloated frameworks; Google Sheets is the CRM.
- Double-layer compliance: deterministic hard rules plus Gemini audit, fail-closed by design.
- Honest by construction: the AI never grades its own deals and never promises what it cannot deliver.

USE CASES:
- Automated sales qualification (SDR Agent)
- Price negotiation with strict compliance (Closer + Finance Agents)
- Real-time CRM updates and deal logging (Zero-Click Execution)
- CRO-level strategic reporting over live CRM data

TECH STACK:
- Python 3.12, Chainlit
- Fireworks AI (DeepSeek-V4-Pro) for reasoning
- Google Gemini (3.5-flash-lite) for compliance auditing
- Google Sheets API for CRM and logging
- Docker + Railway for deployment
