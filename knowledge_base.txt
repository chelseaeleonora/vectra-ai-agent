VECTRA AI - PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE
===================================

PRODUCT NAME: Vectra AI
TYPE: Autonomous Multi-Agent Sales System
TARGET MARKET: Small to Medium Businesses (SMBs)

CORE FEATURES:
1. Multi-Agent Orchestration: Manager, SDR, Closer, and Finance agents work together autonomously.
2. Hybrid LLM Architecture: Uses Fireworks AI (DeepSeek-V4-Flash) for fast reasoning and Google Gemini for strict compliance auditing.
3. Double-Layer Guardrail: Combines AI Audit (Gemini) with Hard-Coded Regex Rules to prevent illegal discounts and hallucinations.
4. Zero-Click Execution: Automatically logs deals and updates CRM status in Google Sheets without human intervention.
5. Inner Monologue UI: Displays real-time agent thinking process for full transparency.

PRICING & DISCOUNT POLICY:
- Maximum discount allowed: 10%
- No negative prices allowed
- No unrealistic promises (e.g., free lifetime upgrades)
- All deals must be audited by Finance Agent before approval

BUSINESS RULES:
1. AI agents must strictly adhere to the 10% discount limit.
2. AI must never hallucinate product features or pricing.
3. All negotiations must be logged in Google Sheets for audit trail.

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE:
- Ultra-lean architecture: No expensive Vector DBs or bloated frameworks.
- Enterprise-grade security with bulletproof guardrails.
- Production-ready with real revenue generation capability.

USE CASES:
- Automated sales qualification (SDR Agent)
- Price negotiation with strict compliance (Closer + Finance Agents)
- Real-time CRM updates and deal logging (Zero-Click Execution)

TECH STACK:
- Python 3.12.9, FastAPI, Chainlit
- Fireworks AI (DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731) for reasoning
- Google Gemini (3.5-flash-lite) for compliance auditing
- Google Sheets API for CRM and logging
